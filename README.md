# Awesome 3D mockups [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Free 3D models, materials, lighting and tools for putting a brand on a 3D product — and the
> licences you need to know before you sell what you make.

Every entry is free (or has a free tier that's genuinely useful). Licences are noted because a
mockup is usually **commercial use** and **changes the model** (your logo on it): check both before
you use anything.

## Make a mockup in your browser

- [TheMark Studio](https://themarkstudio.online) — Put a logo, colours and text on 3D products (packaging, merch, signs, vehicles) or your own GLB; download a picture or GLB. Free, no sign-up; projects stay in your browser. *(Made by us.)*
- [three.js editor](https://threejs.org/editor/) — General 3D scene editor in the browser; import GLB/OBJ/FBX, change materials, export.
- [Babylon.js Sandbox](https://sandbox.babylonjs.com) — Drop a model to view and inspect it with an inspector panel.

## Free 3D models

- [free-3d-mockup-templates](https://github.com/themarkstudio/free-3d-mockup-templates) — 18 blank product models (boxes, cans, bottles, cups, mugs, bags, signs, vehicles) as GLB, true to size. CC0. *(Made by us.)*
- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/models) — High-quality scanned and modelled props, furniture, tools. CC0.
- [Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets) — Reference glTF models (cars, shoes, watches, furniture); licence per model, mostly CC0 / CC BY 4.0; some carry the Khronos logo.
- [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza) — Thousands of low-poly models. CC0 and CC BY.
- [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/search?features=downloadable&type=models) — The largest library; filter by *Downloadable* and by licence. Avoid *NonCommercial* and *NoDerivatives* for mockups.
- [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets) — Game asset packs, many with props and vehicles. CC0.
- [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com) — Low-poly packs. CC0.
- [Smithsonian 3D](https://3d.si.edu) — Museum scans; open-access items are CC0.

## Materials and textures

- [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) — PBR materials (wood, metal, fabric, plastic…). CC0.
- [Poly Haven textures](https://polyhaven.com/textures) — PBR textures. CC0.

## Lighting (HDRIs)

- [Poly Haven HDRIs](https://polyhaven.com/hdris) — Studio and outdoor lighting for realistic reflections. CC0.

## Logos, icons and fonts

- [Iconify](https://icon-sets.iconify.design) — 200,000+ open-source icons from 200+ sets; licence per set. Skip the brand-logo sets: those marks belong to their companies.
- [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org) — Self-hostable open-source fonts (mostly SIL Open Font License).
- [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com) — Open-source fonts, free for commercial use.

## View, check and optimise glTF

- [glTF Viewer](https://gltf-viewer.donmccurdy.com) — Drag-and-drop viewer with validation report.
- [gltf.report](https://gltf.report) — Inspect a GLB's size and run optimisations in the browser.
- [glTF-Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev) — CLI and library to compress (Draco, Meshopt, WebP, KTX2), resize textures and clean up GLB files.
- [gltfpack](https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer) — Fast command-line optimiser from meshoptimizer.
- [glTF Validator](https://github.khronos.org/glTF-Validator/) — The official validator; catches files that won't load.
- [FBX2glTF](https://github.com/godotengine/FBX2glTF) — Convert FBX to glTF/GLB from the command line.

## Licences in one minute

| Licence | Commercial mockups? | Put your logo on it? | Must credit? |
|---------|--------------------|----------------------|--------------|
| CC0 | ✅ | ✅ | No |
| CC BY | ✅ | ✅ | **Yes** — name, licence, link, say what you changed |
| CC BY-SA | ✅ | ✅, but your result must be shared under CC BY-SA too | Yes |
| CC BY-NC (NonCommercial) | ❌ | ✅ for personal use only | Yes |
| CC BY-ND (NoDerivatives) | ✅ as-is only | ❌ — a logo on it is a change | Yes |
| "Editorial" / store licences | Read the terms | Often ❌ | — |

Even with a free licence, **trademarks are separate**: a car model with a manufacturer's badge, or
a can with a real drinks brand, isn't yours to put another brand on. Prefer generic products.
